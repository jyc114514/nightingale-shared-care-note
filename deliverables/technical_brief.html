System boundary

Product thesis

Nightingale is not an EHR replacement, diagnostic system, or autonomous medical decision-maker. AI-scribed content remains a suggestion until clinician review. Display ranking, explicit medical risk, provenance, and human confirmation are separate fields.

Architecture

Server is canonical

Clinic scope and role permissions are enforced on every protected request. No UI-only role switch or database credential reaches the browser.

AI and Voice are bounded

Fixture remains the default. DeepSeek is optional and redaction-gated. Voice is prerecorded synthetic audio with a prepared timestamped transcript; no ASR inference is claimed. Glance reads never call a provider.

Data model / provenance / retrieval policy

Canonical evidence stays intact while views stay glanceable.

The design separates stable record identity, immutable snapshots, source-linked highlights, human review, and derivative read models. Summaries are never the source of truth.

Schema relationships
Exact provenance

Source navigation contract

`source_entry_id` + `source_version_id` + Python Unicode-codepoint offsets + exact quote + SHA-256. The frontend validates `Array.from(text)` and shows an integrity warning instead of an approximate match.

Trust controls

Human decisions remain explicit

Suggested, accepted, rejected, superseded, and conflict-review states remain visible. A clinician can confirm semantic truth, but cannot erase the source record.

final display priority = clamp(base + recency + explicit risk + open action + clinician confirmation + adaptive feedback, 0, 100)

Adaptive feedback is clinic-scoped, idempotent, structured-feature-only, and bounded to [-12, +12]. It changes display priority, never risk or provenance.

Demonstration path
A

Trace and trust

Staff opens Glance, follows an AI-scribed item to its exact immutable source, and keeps suggestion review separate from source content.

B

Collaborate without erasure

Staff edits a role-owned note, selects an @mention, creates an assigned task, and Clinician moves it through its lifecycle while reviewing a plan revision.

C

Surface conflict and history

Two writes use one expected version; the stale write returns 409 and remains beside the winner. Historical context discloses a derived summary that is not the original record and offers labelled immutable original-record rows.

Trade-offs and deferred work
What is implemented: server-side RBAC and clinic scope, immutable revisions, exact provenance, comments/mentions/tasks, metadata-only SSE invalidation, conflict preservation, redaction-gated deterministic AI jobs, prerecorded synthetic audio with prepared timestamped transcript fixtures, bounded adaptive ranking, derived archival context, materialized warm reads, and Render-ready Docker/Blueprint files.
What is not claimed: functional Whisper/ASR or model quality, DeepSeek provider compliance, clinical production readiness, production retention policy, microphone/diarization, or final video content QA before human review.

Safety sentence

AI output is a suggestion. Derived summaries are not canonical sources. Risk is not learned silently. Human review is not replaced by ranking.
